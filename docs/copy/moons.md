**moon** — Always turning the same face toward us, the Moon raises our tides and
was the first world beyond Earth that people walked on.

**ganymede** — The largest moon in the solar system, wider than Mercury, and the
only one known to make its own magnetic field.

**callisto** — The most heavily cratered object we know of, its surface barely
changed in four billion years.

**titan** — The only moon with a thick atmosphere, and the only place besides
Earth with liquid on its surface: rivers and lakes of methane.

**mimas** — One impact crater a third as wide as the moon itself gives Mimas its
unmistakable profile.

**iapetus** — One hemisphere is as dark as coal and the other as bright as snow,
a contrast still not fully explained.

**io** — Squeezed and stretched by Jupiter's gravity, Io is the most volcanically
active body in the solar system.

**europa** — A shell of cracked ice over a salt-water ocean that holds more water
than every sea on Earth.

**enceladus** — Geysers at its south pole spray water from a hidden ocean into
space, feeding one of Saturn's rings.

**phobos** — Spiralling slowly inward, Phobos will break apart or strike Mars
within about fifty million years.

**deimos** — The smaller Martian moon is so distant and faint that from the
surface it looks like a bright star.
