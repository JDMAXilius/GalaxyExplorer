# Hint cards

Two cards, shown once after the intro places the Earth pin, in the style of the
intro's own prompts. Each dismisses when the player performs the action or after
six seconds. Replayable from the dock's Help button. ASCII only.

---

## hint_grab
**Title:** Pinch to grab
**Line:** Reach out, put your finger and thumb together, and pull a planet toward you.
**Animation:** A hand pinching a small planet and drawing it in (3 s loop).
**Dismisses on:** the first successful grab.

---

## hint_scale
**Title:** Two hands to resize
**Line:** Pinch with both hands and move them apart to make what you are holding bigger.
**Animation:** Two hands pinching and separating around a planet (3 s loop).
**Dismisses on:** the first two-hand scale.
